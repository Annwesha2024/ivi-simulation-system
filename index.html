How the IVI System Works

  1. The driver interacts with the IVI HMI (touch, voice, or buttons).
  2. The IVI Controller / Middleware receives and routes every command.
  3. Media Service manages audio playback and streaming sources.
  4. Navigation Service combines GPS data and Map Database data to plan routes.
  5. Projection Service turns route/media data into something that can be displayed.
  6. Audio HAL sends processed audio to the Vehicle Speakers.
  7. Display HAL sends UI/video output to the Central Display or HUD.
  8. The Vehicle Network (CAN/LIN) connects the IVI system with Vehicle ECUs.
  9. A connected smartphone can provide Android Auto / Apple CarPlay projection.
